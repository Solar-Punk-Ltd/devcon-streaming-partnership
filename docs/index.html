Where the design stands

Swarm is the origin, the storage and the archive. Every stage is published twice, on two lanes sharing no signing key, no feed and no postage batch, so a forked feed is impossible by construction rather than policed.

A cache sits in front of nodes we run, because content-addressed segments are immutably cacheable and that is what makes a conference audience affordable. Anyone who prefers can fetch the identical hash-verified bytes from the network directly.
